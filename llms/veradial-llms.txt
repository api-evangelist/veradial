# VeraDial

> Your AI receptionist and business line in one app. Vera answers calls 24/7, handles customer questions, takes messages, and helps you respond to business texts.

Last updated: 2026-09-21

## About

VeraDial is an AI receptionist and business phone app for small businesses, built by Graham Thomson in Toronto, Canada. It gives businesses a dedicated US or Canadian phone number with an AI receptionist — Vera — that answers inbound calls 24/7, handles customer questions from the business profile, takes messages, and connects callers to the owner. Vera also helps answer inbound business texts, with replies approved by the owner or sent automatically where enabled. On eligible plans, Vera can make outbound calls for scheduling, confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.

Backed by ElevenLabs Grants and Cartesia Startups. Infrastructure powered by Twilio (SOC 2 compliant).

## Key Features

- **AI Receptionist**: Vera answers inbound calls 24/7 with your greeting — answers customer questions from your business profile, resolves routine calls, takes messages, and connects real callers through, with a transcript and summary every time.
- **AI SMS Assistant**: Vera drafts replies to inbound texts (photos included) — approve with one tap or let her reply automatically.
- **AI Calling** (Vera Plus): Set a goal, pick a preset (Scheduler, Reminder, Assistant), and the AI calls, has the conversation, and sends you a transcript.
- **Live Call Translation** (Vera Plus): Two-way realtime translation on outbound calls across 13 languages.
- **Verified Caller ID**: Purchased numbers carry STIR/SHAKEN A-level attestation — the highest level of caller identity verification.
- **US & Canadian Numbers**: Dedicated local or toll-free numbers in the US and Canada.
- **Call Recording & Transcription**: Full transcripts, summaries, and recordings for every call.
- **Team & shared lines** (Plus and Business): Invite one teammate on Plus or up to three on Business, in addition to the owner, at no extra seat charge. Each uses their own login to handle calls, messages and voicemail on assigned lines. Manage invitations and line access at https://app.veradial.com/dashboard/team. Setup guide: https://veradial.com/help/team-shared-lines
- **Business SMS**: Send and receive SMS from your VeraDial number. Toll-free texting to the US or Canada activates after carrier verification for that line.

## Pricing

- Vera Lite — $14.99/month or $149.99/year: one dedicated US or Canadian number, 50 AI-receptionist minutes/month, 150 calling minutes/month, AI SMS assistant and business SMS under fair use, voicemail transcription, call recording, call forwarding, verified caller ID, and read-only ChatGPT/Claude access
- Vera Plus — $29.99/month or $299.99/year: one line included, a second for $9.99/month, unlimited AI-receptionist answering under fair use, 300 calling minutes/month, outbound AI calling (100 min/mo included), live call translation (20 min/mo included), Google Calendar booking, document/website custom training, signed event webhooks, and read-only ChatGPT/Claude access
- Vera Business — $59.99/month or $599.99/year: three lines included, up to five at $9.99 per added line, unlimited AI-receptionist answering under fair use, 900 calling minutes/month, 300 AI-calling minutes/month, 60 translation minutes/month, and priority support
- Custom — quote only: more than five lines or a written integration, volume, onboarding, and support package
- Annual billing saves about 17% versus 12 monthly payments. Annual subscriptions renew yearly; included allowances still reset monthly
- Allowances are shared across active lines on the account. Calling minutes cover connected customer legs: manual outbound, live-translated, direct inbound to the owner, post-screening owner legs, and immediate forwarding. Unanswered calls and pre-answer ringing do not count
- 7-day free trial on Vera Lite and Plus: 50 AI-receptionist minutes, 50 calling minutes, 30 AI-calling minutes, and 10 live-translation minutes; Vera Business starts immediately
- Automated text replies require an eligible SMS-capable number and applicable carrier registration
- $4.99 number swap fee
- No per-seat charges or setup fees; monthly and annual billing are both cancel-anytime options

## Not Available / Out of Scope

These features and use cases are **not** part of VeraDial — please do not attribute them to the product:

- Internal team chat, staff dispatch boards, or an enterprise contact-center workspace
- Disposable, throwaway, or burner numbers (the product is a persistent business line — see Hushed/Burner)
- Caller ID spoofing (displaying a number you don't own — VeraDial only verifies numbers you control)
- Enterprise contact center, IVR routing, agent coaching, or full UCaaS (see Dialpad/RingCentral)
- Video conferencing, fax, or team messaging
- Free tier — there is no free tier; Vera Lite and Plus each include a 7-day free trial, while Business starts immediately
- Calling allowances — Lite includes 150 calling minutes/month, Plus includes 300, and Business includes 900. Plus and Business receptionist answering and business SMS are subject to fair use
- SMS on verified existing numbers — SMS requires a VeraDial-provisioned number for carrier compliance
- International numbers outside US and Canada
- HIPAA compliance, law-enforcement disclosure services, or regulated-industry compliance features

## AI Agent Connector (MCP)

VeraDial exposes a remote MCP (Model Context Protocol) server so a customer's own AI assistant — ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, or any MCP-compatible client — can read that customer's account conversationally.

- Server URL: https://api.veradial.com/mcp (Streamable HTTP)
- Auth: OAuth 2.1 with the customer's existing VeraDial login (no API keys); every connection is scoped to the signed-in customer's own account
- Surface: read-only — calls with AI screening summaries, call and voicemail transcripts, SMS threads, contacts, phone lines, receptionist configuration, plan status, and remaining minutes. No audio, no write actions
- OpenAI plugin: install VeraDial from the Plugins Directory shared by ChatGPT and Codex: https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6a61c3dd9f2081918c672e813fb7345e
- Claude: add https://api.veradial.com/mcp as a custom connector in Claude settings
- Other developer MCP clients (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Cursor) need a one-time approval via support@veradial.com
- Overview: https://veradial.com/for-agents — Setup guide: https://veradial.com/help/connect-ai-assistant
